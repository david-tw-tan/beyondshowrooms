Our process

  1. 1

    Tell me about your home

    Start with me in Germany. If you have them, share floor plans, photos, a budget, and the rooms that matter. The first conversation is free. We get to know each other, and I’ll answer questions before David builds the Foshan plan.

  2. 2

    Create your plan

    A planning fee of US$500 starts this: David matches you to the right factories for your style and budget, shows you their collections, and delivers an itemized furniture list with updated floor plans. I stay in the loop so nothing gets lost between Germany and Foshan.

  3. 3

    Review plan. Place order

    Come to Foshan to see pieces in person before ordering, or order remotely. Either way, your final cost is the factory sales price, plus shipping, duties, and installation. The service fee is included in that price.

* In-person Foshan visits are charged separately: US$150 a day (car, driver, guide). Most trips average 3–4 days.

Post-order, from factory to home

Once you confirm an order, David stays involved on the Foshan side until every piece is installed—all covered by the service fee. I remain your point of contact in Germany:

i Production — Typically 6 to 8 weeks; every item is made to order.

ii Final inspection — I send you photos of all pieces for approval before they’re shipped.

iii Shipping — All items consolidated into a single container, so everything ships together.

iv Last-mile delivery — White-glove placement and installation.

Overseas aftercare

My core factories all cover replacement parts—free the first year, then at cost. Full unit replacements are for China-only orders.

Curated network of trustworthy factories

Foshan has thousands of furniture factories. I’ve done the legwork—visiting production lines and meeting the owners. I work only with the truly reliable ones, not showroom theater.

My core set includes 25+ export-grade factories, chosen for both product quality and aftercare service. Some cover broad categories: living, dining, and bedroom. Others are specialists, focused on a single furniture type such as luxury dining tables or outdoor furniture.

Every factory has its strengths and weaknesses, and also operates at different price/quality tiers. I’ll candidly tell you which factory excels in which furniture type and styles. Unlike agents who do factory tours with minimal guidance, you’ll get real insider advice and recommendations when working with me.
