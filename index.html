My process

What happens when you hire me, in plain terms.

  1. 1

    Tell me about your home

    Share floor plans, photos, a budget, and the rooms that matter. The first conversation is free. I’ll answer basic questions. If we’re not a good fit, I’ll tell you.

  2. 2

    Create your plan

    A project fee of $750 starts this: I match you to the right factories, show you their staged collections, and deliver an itemized furniture list with updated floor plans.

  3. 3

    Review plan, place order

    Come to Foshan to see pieces in person before ordering, or order remotely. Either way, your final cost is the direct factory price, plus shipping, duties, and installation.

Post-order, from factory to home

Once you confirm an order, I stay involved until every piece is installed in your home:

i Production — Typically 6 to 8 weeks; every item is made to order.

ii Final inspection — I send you photos of all pieces for approval before they’re shipped.

iii Shipping — All items consolidated into a single container, so everything ships together.

iv Last-mile delivery — White-glove placement and installation.

Overseas aftercare

Factories cover replacement parts—free the first year, then at cost. Full unit replacements are for China-only orders.

Curated network of trustworthy factories

My core set includes 25+ export-grade factories, chosen for both product quality and aftercare service. Some cover broad categories: living, dining, and bedroom. Others are specialists, focused on a single furniture type such as luxury dining tables or outdoor furniture.

Every factory has its particular strengths. Within my network, different factories also operate at different price/quality tiers. I’ll candidly suggest the right mix for your target style and budget.
